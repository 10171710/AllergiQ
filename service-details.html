Allergen Immunotherapy (SCIT & SLIT)

The only FDA-approved clinical method that retrains your immune system to develop lifelong natural tolerance.

  1. Immunotherapy Details

What is Allergen Immunotherapy?

Allergen immunotherapy is a disease-modifying medical treatment that works by introducing tiny, escalating doses of the exact allergen extracts your body is reactive against. Over time, your immune system shifts from creating inflammatory IgE antibodies (which trigger histamine release, nasal swelling, and wheezing) to producing protective IgG4 blocking antibodies.

Clinical Efficacy Milestone

According to extensive AAAAI clinical trials, over 85% to 92% of patients experience a dramatic reduction or complete cessation of medication dependency after completing their 3-year immunotherapy cycle.

The 4-Phase Dose Escalation Protocol

AllergiQ uses standard CLIA-certified serial dilution compounding. Patients progress through 4 distinct color-coded vials:

Vial Stage Dilution Level Frequency Clinical Objective
Vial 1 (Silver) 1:10,000 Concentrated Weekly In-Clinic Shot Initial priming of cellular immune receptors
Vial 2 (Blue) 1:1,000 Concentrated Weekly In-Clinic Shot Gradual titration; minimal localized redness
Vial 3 (Yellow) 1:100 Concentrated Weekly In-Clinic Shot High-potency build-up; symptom reduction starts
Vial 4 (Red) 1:1 Full Maintenance Every 3 to 4 Weeks Long-term protective IgG4 immune memory (3-5 Years)
