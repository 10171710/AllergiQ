Our Medical Mission

We Do Not Just Treat Symptoms. We Cure Sensitivities.

At AllergiQ, we believe that no one should have their life restricted by airborne pollen, pet dander, food allergies, or recurring allergic asthma. Our clinic unites fellowship-trained allergists, certified nurses, and cutting-edge molecular diagnostics to deliver personalized, permanent immune tolerance.

Precision Diagnostics
Component-level IgE testing isolating exact molecular allergen proteins.
Custom Immunotherapy
Compounded antigen serums tailored to each patient's exact sensitivity.
