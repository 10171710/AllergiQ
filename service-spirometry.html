About Pulmonary Spirometry & Asthma

Spirometry objectively measures how much air your lungs can hold and how forcefully you can exhale it (FEV1/FVC). We record baseline values, then repeat the test after a bronchodilator to reveal whether your airways are reversibly narrowed - the hallmark of asthma.

Why Patients Choose This Service

Objective lung numbers let us stage asthma severity precisely and confirm whether biologic therapy is appropriate for you.

What to Expect

Digital FEV1/FVC loop captured and trended over time
Pre- and post-bronchodilator reversibility testing
Asthma severity staging and inhaler technique review
Screening for advanced therapy such as biologics
