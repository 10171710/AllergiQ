Comparing Diagnostic Testing Modalities

Understanding which testing method aligns with your specific allergic symptoms.

Diagnostic Method Time to Results Discomfort Level Antihistamine Hold Required? Best Clinical Indication
Skin Prick Testing (SPT) 15 - 20 Minutes Very Mild (Tickle) Yes (5-7 Days) Airborne pollens, pet dander, primary food triggers
Serum Specific IgE (ImmunoCAP) 24 - 48 Hours Routine Blood Draw No (Can continue meds) Severe eczema patients, dermatographism, high risk
48-Hour Patch Testing 48 - 72 Hours Painless (Adhesive) No Contact dermatitis, metals (nickel), cosmetic preservatives
Intradermal Skin Testing 15 Minutes Mild Pinch Yes (5-7 Days) Insect venom allergies and drug (penicillin) testing
Oral Food Challenge (OFC) 2 - 4 Hours Under MD Care Yes (5 Days) Definitive confirmation of food allergy outgrowths

Preparing for Your Allergy Skin Test

To ensure 100% accurate diagnostic results, please discontinue all oral antihistamines (Zyrtec, Allegra, Claritin, Benadryl) 5 to 7 days before your appointment.

Continue prescribed asthma inhalers
Continue saline nasal sprays
Stop sleep aids with diphenhydramine
Wear comfortable short-sleeved clothing
